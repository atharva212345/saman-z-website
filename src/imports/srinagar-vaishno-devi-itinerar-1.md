DESTINATION OVERVIEW
Srinagar
The heart of Kashmir, Srinagar offers enchanting Mughal gardens, tranquil lakes, and traditional
houseboat living. A Shikara ride across Dal Lake and visits to historical gardens create a serene
introduction to the valley.
Sonmarg
Known as the Meadow of Gold, Sonmarg boasts glacier views, alpine meadows, and pristine rivers,
making it a spectacular day excursion.
Pahalgam
Nestled along the Lidder River, Pahalgam is famous for lush valleys, pine forests, and cinematic
landscapes including Betaab Valley and Aru Valley.
Gulmarg
A premier hill resort renowned for skiing slopes and the world-famous Gondola cable car ride, Gulmarg
features sweeping mountain vistas.
Doodhpathri
A lesser-explored meadow destination offering tranquil landscapes and flowing streams — ideal for
relaxation and photography.
Katra – Vaishno Devi
Gateway to one of India’s holiest shrines. The pilgrimage trek provides a deeply spiritual experience and
divine blessings.

DAY-WISE ITINERARY
Day 01 — Arrival Srinagar | Local Sightseeing
Upon arrival in Srinagar, meet our representative and transfer to the hotel. After freshening up,
proceed for local sightseeing.
Srinagar – Destination Information
Srinagar, the summer capital of Jammu & Kashmir, is renowned for its Mughal gardens, scenic
lakes, and traditional Kashmiri culture. It offers a blend of natural beauty and historical charm.
Sightseeing Highlights
Mughal Gardens
Nishat Bagh — Terrace garden overlooking Dal Lake
Chashme Shahi — Natural spring garden
Tulip Garden — Tulip Festival Garden Main Attraction.
Shankaracharya Temple (Optional) — Panoramic city views
Dal Lake Visit & Shikara Ride Included In package — Traditional wooden boat experience
Dinner & Overnight stay at Srinagar Hotel
Day 02 — Sonmarg Day Excursion
After breakfast, drive to Sonmarg.
Sonmarg – Destination Information
Known as the “Meadow of Gold”, Sonmarg sits amidst majestic glaciers and alpine meadows
and serves as a gateway to Ladakh.
Sightseeing
Arrival at Sonmarg and transfer to local taxi (direct payment basis)
Excursion towards Gumri / Zero Point (subject to weather & road permissions)
Scenic drive along Sindh River
Thajiwas Glacier (pony ride optional, direct payment)
Snow & mountain landscapes
Return to Srinagar
Dinner & Overnight stay at Hotel
Day 03 — Pahalgam Day Excursion
Breakfast at hotel and depart for Pahalgam.
Pahalgam – Destination Information
A picturesque valley located on the banks of Lidder River, famous for lush pine forests and
valleys used as Bollywood shooting locations.
Sightseeing
Enroute saffron fields & Awantipora ruins
Betaab Valley (optional union cab)
Aru Valley (optional union cab)
Lidder River walk & local market
Return to Srinagar
Dinner & Overnight stay
Day 04 — Gulmarg Day Excursion
After breakfast drive to Gulmarg.
Gulmarg – Destination Information
Popular hill resort known for snow sports, meadows, and one of the highest cable cars in the
world.
Sightseeing
Gulmarg Meadow exploration
Gondola Cable Car Ride (optional)
Snow activities / photography
St. Mary’s Church
Return to Srinagar
Dinner & Overnight stay
Day 05 — Doodhpathri Excursion | Houseboat Stay
After breakfast check out and proceed for excursion.
Doodhpathri – Destination Information
A hidden gem known as the “Valley of Milk”, featuring green meadows, pine forests, and milky-
flowing streams.
Sightseeing:
Meadow walks
Riverside relaxation
Nature photography
Return to Srinagar and check in to Deluxe Houseboat. Evening free to enjoy:Dal Lake ambience
Local handicraft shopping
Dinner & Overnight stay in Houseboat
Day 06 — Srinagar to Katra | Vaishno Devi Darshan
After breakfast transfer to Srinagar Railway Station.
Train Journey
Board Vande Bharat Train to Katra.
Arrival at Katra, pickup and transfer to hotel.
Vaishno Devi – Destination Information
One of India’s most sacred pilgrimages dedicated to Goddess Durga, located in the Trikuta
Mountains.
Evening proceed for Darshan (by walk/pony/helicopter — optional arrangements).
Overnight stay at Katra Hotel
Day 07 — Katra | Rest / Leisure
After breakfast, full day free for rest after darshan.
Optional Activities:
Local market visit
Temple revisit
Relaxation
Dinner & Overnight stay at Katra
Day 08 — Departure Jammu Airport
After breakfast check out and transfer to Jammu Airport for onward journey.
