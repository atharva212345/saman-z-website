Travel Itinerary
Day 01 Arrival at Jaipur | Welcome to the 'Pink City of India'
• Reach Jaipur where you will be meeting a representative who will help you get transferred
to the hotel.
• On reaching the hotel, complete the check-in process and take some rest.
• In the evening you can proceed on an optional tour to the Laxminarayan Temple also
known as Birla Mandir.
• You will then proceed to ChokhiDhani, which is an ethnic village resort and here you can
enjoy a camel ride and a puppet show.
• Later you will proceed back to your hotel.
• Overnight stay at the hotel in Jaipur.
Day 02 Sightseeing in Jaipur | Explore Amber Fort Sheesh Mahal, a Heartthrob of
Bollywood Films
• Have a hearty breakfast before embarking on the full day sightseeing tour to Jaipur.
• Visit the grand Amber Fort and Palace cradled on the top of the Aravali Hill overlooking the
pink city of Jaipur.
• Then proceed to Gaitore which is not only a royal cremation ground of KachhwahaRajputs
but also an architectural marvel.
• On your way back, stopover at the beautiful Man Sagar Lake and admire the grand Jal
Mahal palace made of Red Sandstone.
• Then explore the rich history of Jaipur as you proceed to City Palace and Museum and
JantarMantar, which is a heritage site with fixed instruments for making astronomical
observations.
• Then proceed for a photo stop at Hawa Mahal and you can also opt to explore the nearby
markets.
• Later in the evening you will proceed back to your hotel.
• Overnight stay at the hotel in Jaipur.
Day 03 Jaipur - Pushkar | One and only Bramha Temple in India
• Wake up in the morning, have a lip smacking breakfast, check-out from the hotel and enjoy
a road trip to Pushkar. 145km journey will take rughly 3 hours.
• As you reach Pushkar, check-in at the pre-booked hotel and get some rest.
• Later, proceed for local sightseeing to visit historic Pushkar temple and Anad sagar lake.
• return to the hotel for dinner.
• Overnight stay in Pushkar.
Day 04 Pushkar to Jodhpur | Visit to one of the strongest forts in India
• Wake up in the morning, have delicious breakfast and complete the checkout formalities
from the hotel.
• Start your Journey to Jodhpur on arrival check in to hotel and Visit Jodhpur.
• Start from the Umaid Bhawan Palace Museum that houses some antique clocks, art deco
furniture, dining and writing sets, etc.
• Witness the shades of this blue city from the top of the Mehrangarh Fort that is situated
on a low sandstone hill.
• Within the fort visit Moti Mahal and Phool Mahal for their intricate interior designs
• Also, visit JaswantThada - an imposing marble cenotaph built in memory of Maharaja
Jaswant Singh II around 1899.
• Return back to your hotel for dinner.
• Overnight stay in Jodhpur.
Day 05 Jodhpur to Jaisalmer | Travel to the Shimmering 'Golden City' of Rajasthan.
• Start your next day with a delicious breakfast then get ready to transfer to Jaisalmer, also
known as the Golden City.
• Take a short stopover at the Jaisalmer War Memorial, which honors the contributions and
sacrifices of war heroes, and also traces the evolution of the Indian Army.
• On arrival in Jaisalmer, check in to your hotel and take some rest.
• The rest of the day is at leisure and you are free to explore the colorful markets and fairs.
• Once you are done exploring the markets, return back to the hotel.
• Overnight stay at the hotel in Jaisalmer.
Day 06 Sightseeing in Jaisalmer | Explore the Secrets Hidden Behind the Beautiful Sand
Dunes
• After breakfast, you can proceed to uncover the secrets hidden behind the Golden Sands
of the Thar Desert in Jaisalmer.
• You can visit the golden Jaisalmer Fort that looks like it has just risen from the sands with
its golden limestone walls.
• And then, you can proceed to marvel at the beautiful royal Havelis including Patwon-Ki-
Haveli, Nathmal-Ki-Haveli, and Salim Singh-Ki-Haveli that speak of the royal past.
• Dating back from the 12th to 15th century, these Havelis feature exquisite carvings and
filigree work.
• After experiencing the stunning architectural marvels you can take a short stopover at the
beautiful Gadisar Lake that was once the only source of water in Jaisalmer.
• You can then depart for a camel ride to the beautiful Sam Sand Dunes overlooking a
spectacular sunset.
• After a wonderful camel safari, you can proceed to check in to the desert camps and enjoy
folk performances by the locals while enjoying a bonfire.
• Have a scrumptious and authentic Rajasthani meal.
• Overnight stay at the Desert Camp in Jaisalmer.
Day 07 Jaisalmer to Jodhpur and Departure | End of Trip with a Bag Full of Life Time
Memories
• Wake up at your camps to a beautiful morning in the desert and enjoy a delicious breakfast.
• Check out from the desert camps after packing your bags along with a bundle of
memories.
• You can then proceed back towards Jodhpur.
• Conclude the tour with beautiful memories on reaching your desired destination in
Jodhpur for your onward journey.
