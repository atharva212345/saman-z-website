Green Kerala 06 Nights & 7 Days
Munnar 2N, Thekkady, 1N , Alleppy 1N, kovalam 2N, Day Trip Kanyakumari
Day 1- Arrival at Cochin Airport driver will be waiting for you at exit gate. Board vehicle and Transfer to Munnar
and on arrival in Munnar, Hotel checks in &Munnar local sightseeing.
This Hill Station is located at an attitude of 1600m above sea level. Munnar offers an excellent view of Tea, Coffee
& cardamom plantations the rolling hills, the lush mountains & the breath taking scenery among the exotic flora
found in the forest. Munnar also having highest peak in South India, Anamudi, which towers 2695 m.
Eravikulam National Park:
Start your day early by visiting Eravikulam National Park, which is famous for the NilgiriTahr (a species of
mountain goat). The park is also home to lush green hills and offers a stunning view of the Anamudi Peak, the
highest peak in South India.You can take a shuttle ride from the base area to the top of the park for great views.
Spice Garden visit (It can be on extra chargeable basis).
Chocolate Factory visit.
Stay at Munnar with Dinner.
Day 2-After Breakfast proceed to Munnar sightseeing,
Mattupetty Dam: Visit Mattupetty Dam, located around 13 km from Munnar. It's a picturesque spot where you
can enjoy boating. The nearby area offers a beautiful landscape for photography.
Echo Point: After the dam, head to Echo Point, where you can hear your voice reverberate in the mountains,
surrounded by misty hills.
Day 3- After breakfast transfer to Thekkady (3 ½ Hrs.). En route visit Cardamom Mountains & Tea plantations
(Tea Garden Visit) . Arrival at Thekkady Hotel check in & evening enjoy at Spice market at Thekkady. Evening you
can enjoy Kathakali show (Folk show). Thekkady Night stay.
Day 4- Early Morning explore Periyaar Wild life safari through a boat ride at 07.00 am to 09.00 am at your
own cost. Our driver will drop you at Periyaar WLS, Boat Center. Tickets for boat ride are available here.
Periyaar one of the well-known Wild Life Sanctuaries in the south, attracts a large number of nature lovers. Also
called as Thekkady WLS, which is an ideal place to watch animals in their natural habitat. Back to hotel and
after breakfast check out from hotel and transfer to Allepey on arrival check in to houseboat and explore
Alleppy Backwaters by houseboat ride on your own cost..AllepeyHotel stay with Lunch and dinner.
Day 5-After Breakfast check out from hotel and proceed to Jatayu Earth’s center a full day sightseeing. This Day
tour to Jatayu Earth's Center Visit. Jatayu Park is a massive nature park in Kerala. Jatayu Earth Center is the
world’s largest bird statue or sculpture. After JatayuTransfer to kovalam by road, kovalam arrival and check in to
hotel and Kovlam Night stay. (Enjoy evening on Kovalam beach)
Day 6-After Breakfast Check out from hotel and proceed to Kanyakumari day Trip which is 2 hours road trip one
side, visit Kanyakumarimata temple, Vivekanand rock memorialect.. Evening return back to Kovalam .
Day 7- After breakfast by road transfer to Cochin and Drop at Cochin Airport
