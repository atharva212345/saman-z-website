Chitwan (2N) – Pokhara (2N)
A perfect blend of heritage, wildlife, spirituality, and Himalayan landscapes, this Nepal tour
covers UNESCO World Heritage Sites in Kathmandu, thrilling jungle experiences in Chitwan
National Park, and scenic beauty in Pokhara.
Day-wise Detailed Itinerary
Day 01: Arrival at Kathmandu & Local Sightseeing
Upon arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport, Kathmandu, you will be greeted by our
representative and transferred to your hotel for check-in.
Later, proceed for half-day sightseeing:
• Kathmandu Durbar Square – A UNESCO World Heritage Site showcasing ancient
palaces, courtyards, and temples reflecting traditional Newari architecture.
• Swayambhunath Stupa (Monkey Temple) – One of the oldest Buddhist stupas in
Nepal, offering panoramic views of Kathmandu Valley.
Evening free for leisure, local market exploration, or rest. Overnight stay: Kathmandu
Day 02: Kathmandu → Chitwan National Park (Approx. 5 hrs drive)
After breakfast, drive to Chitwan National Park, Nepal’s first national park and a UNESCO
World Heritage Site, famous for its wildlife and jungle experiences.
On arrival, check in at the resort and enjoy lunch. Post check-in, the resort staff will brief
you about the upcoming jungle activities.
In the evening, enjoy the vibrant Tharu Cultural Dance Show, showcasing the traditions of
the indigenous Tharu community.
Dinner and overnight stay at the resort.
Note: All jungle activities in Chitwan are arranged by the resort and conducted on a sharing
basis.
Day 03: Chitwan – Jungle Activities
After breakfast, get ready for an adventurous day in the jungle:
• Jeep Safari – Explore the dense forests of Chitwan National Park with chances to
spot rhinos, deer, wild boar, crocodiles, and exotic birds.
• Canoe Ride – A peaceful ride along the Rapti River, ideal for bird watching and
spotting crocodiles.
Rest of the day at leisure. Dinner and overnight stay at the resort.
Day 04: Chitwan → Pokhara (Approx. 6 hrs drive)
After breakfast, drive towards Pokhara, the scenic lake city nestled in the Annapurna range.
En route, visit:
• Manakamana Temple – A revered Hindu temple dedicated to Goddess Bhagwati,
accessible by cable car (ticket at own cost).
Continue the drive to Pokhara. On arrival, check in at the hotel. Evening free to stroll around
Lakeside Market.
Overnight stay: Pokhara
Day 05: Pokhara City Tour & Sunrise at Sarangkot
Early morning around 05:00 AM, drive to Sarangkot (approx. 25 minutes) to witness a
breathtaking sunrise over the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri mountain ranges.
Return to the hotel for breakfast, then proceed for full-day city sightseeing covering:
• Devi’s Fall – A unique waterfall that disappears into an underground tunnel.
• Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave – A sacred cave temple dedicated to Lord Shiva.
• Pumdikot Shiva Statue – One of the tallest Shiva statues, offering panoramic valley
views.
• Phewa Lake – Enjoy a serene boat ride to Tal Barahi Temple, located on an island.
• Bindabasini Temple – A significant Hindu temple dedicated to Goddess Durga.
Return to hotel. Overnight stay: Pokhara
Day 06: Pokhara → Kathmandu (Approx. 8 hrs drive)
After breakfast, drive back to Kathmandu, enjoying scenic river valleys and countryside
views en route.
On arrival, check in at the hotel. Evening free for shopping at Thamel, leisure activities, or
exploring local cafes.
Overnight stay: Kathmandu
Day 07: Kathmandu Sightseeing & Departure
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and visit:
• Pashupatinath Temple – Nepal’s most sacred Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva
(non-Hindus can view from outside).
• Guheshwori Temple (Shakti Peeth) – One of the most important temples dedicated
to Goddess Parvati.
Later, transfer to the airport for your onward journey with memorable experiences of
Nepal.
