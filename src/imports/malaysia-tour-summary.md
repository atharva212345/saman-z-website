TENTATIVE TOUR ITINERARY
Day 1: Arrival
Upon your arrival in Kuala lumper. Free time to leisure. Overnight stay at hotel. Overnight at the hotel.
DAY 2: CITY TOUR + KLCC + BATU CAVES (B/-/D)
After breakfast proceed to visit Batu caves. half day Kuala lumpur city tour, this is an interesting tour which
unfolds the beauty and charm of an old and new Kuala Lumpur. Kuala Lumpur is rich with its history on how it
developed from a small tin Miners Settlement to a growing modern city currently. Later we will proceed to visit
visit KLCC Aquarium Featuring 60,000 square feet (5,600 m2) in two levels with a 90-metre (300 ft) underwater
tunnel, Aquaria KLCC houses over 250 different species and over 5,000 land and aquatic animals from Malasiya
and around the world. Interactive information kiosks on fish and turtle conservation. It includes a themed retail
area of about 5,000 square feet (460 m2). Aquaria KLCC are based on the journey of water from the land to the
sea. The journey starts in the misty highlands, down through rivers, through the rainforest and mangroves to the
coral reefs into the deep blue sea. Overnight at the hotel.
Day 3: BATU CAVES + GENTING HIGHLAND (B/-/D)
After breakfast proceed to Enroute visit Batu caves. then proceed to Genting highland. Get out of the city for a
day of entertainment and fun with this excursion to Genting Highlands. The popular theme park and hill resort
is home to a $D cinema casino, splash pool and plenty of games and rides---including Southeast Asia’s fastest and
longest cable car. You’ll enjoy six hours of free time to explore all of the highlights of Malaysia’s largest casino
and resort. Overnight at the hotel.
Day 4: MALAYSIA – MUMBAI (B/-/-)
After breakfast @ hotel. Later today, proceed to airport as this fantastic tour comes an end. Return home with
wonderful memories of your tour with Samanz Holidays
