SAMAN'Z HOLIDAYS
Duration: 5 Nights / 6 Days
Destinations: Chandigarh – Shimla - Manali
Day 1: Chandigarh to Shimla (140 Kms / Approx. 4–5 Hours)
Upon arrival at Chandigarh Railway Station/Airport, our representative will greet you and assist with
your transfer to Shimla, the former summer capital of British India.
Enjoy a scenic drive through beautiful hills, pine forests, and winding mountain roads.
After arrival:Check-in at the hotelFreshen up and relax
Evening Visit:
Mall Road – The heart of Shimla, lined with colonial buildings, cafes, shops, and restaurants.
Lakkar Bazaar – Famous for wooden handicrafts and souvenirs.
Enjoy the cool mountain breeze and vibrant local atmosphere.
Dinner and overnight stay at the hotel in Shimla.
Day 2: Shimla Local Sightseeing
After breakfast, proceed for a full-day excursion around Shimla.
1. Kufri Excursion
Visit Kufri, a popular hill station known for:
Snow activities (in winter)
Horse riding (optional)
Himalayan views
Explore:
Indira Tourist Park
Himalayan Nature Park (Mini Zoo) – Home to Himalayan wildlife like musk deer and snow leopards.
2. Local Attractions in Shimla
Jakhoo Temple – Ancient temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman, offering panoramic city views.
Christ Church – Second oldest church in North India with stunning neo-Gothic architecture.
The Ridge – A large open space with spectacular mountain views.
Evening free for:
Shopping on Mall Road
Trying local Himachali cuisine
Dinner and overnight stay at the hotel.
Day 3: Shimla to Manali (250 Kms / Approx. 7–8 Hours)
After breakfast, check out and proceed towards Manali.
Drive through:
Scenic Kullu Valley
River Beas flowing alongside the highway
Lush green mountains and apple orchards
En route you may visit:
Pandoh Dam
Kullu – Famous for shawls and river rafting (optional at own cost)
Arrival in Manali:
Check-in at hotel
Relax and enjoy the cool mountain climate
Dinner and overnight stay in Manali.
Day 4: Manali Local Sightseeing
After breakfast, explore the charm of Manali.
1. Hadimba Devi Temple
Visit Hadimba Devi Temple, a 16th-century wooden temple surrounded by cedar forests.
2. Vashisht Village
Explore Vashisht Village, famous for: Natural hot water springs Ancient temples
3. Club House
Managed by HPTDC, offers indoor games and riverside relaxation.
4. Tibetan Monasteries
Visit beautiful monasteries showcasing Tibetan culture and handicrafts.
5. Van Vihar
A peaceful park with tall deodar trees near Mall Road.
Evening:
Explore Manali Mall Road
Shop for woollens, handicrafts, and souvenirs
Dinner and overnight stay at the hotel.
Day 5: Solang Valley – Atal Tunnel – (Optional Rohtang Pass)
After breakfast, proceed for an adventurous day.
Solang Valley
Visit Solang Valley, a paradise for adventure lovers:
Paragliding
Zorbing
Ropeway ride
Snow activities (seasonal)
Atal Tunnel
Drive through the engineering marvel Atal Tunnel, one of the world's longest high-altitude tunnels
connecting Manali to Lahaul Valley.
Optional: Rohtang Pass (Subject to Permit & Extra Cost)
Visit Rohtang Pass, known for: Snow-covered landscapes.Stunning Himalayan views
Snow activities
Return to hotel by evening.
Dinner and overnight stay in Manali.
Day 6: Manali to Chandigarh (Approx. 7–8 Hours)
After breakfast:
Check out from hotel
Drive back to Chandigarh
Drop at Railway Station/Airport for onward journey with beautiful memories of Himachal Pradesh.
